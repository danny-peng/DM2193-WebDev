# Sausage Egg and Cheese Sandwich

Adapted from [NYT Cooking](https://cooking.nytimes.com/recipes/1025303-sausage-egg-and-cheese-sandwich)

A **popular** *quick and easy* breakfast that only takes around 20 minutes to make. Perfect for busy people who don't want to skip breakfast.

---
## Ingredients
- 1 Split English Muffin
- Unsalted Butter
- 4 Ounces of Italian Sausage, Casings Removed
- 1 Large Egg
- Salt and Black Pepper
- 1 Slice American Cheese

## Instructions
1. Toast the English Muffin, spread butter on cut sides
2. Roll sausage into a ball. Heat a cast-iron skillet over medium-heat. Add the sausage and press down with spatula until 1/4-inch thick. Cook until browned and crisp, about 2 minutes per side. Transfer to bottom of muffin and turn off heat.
3. Wipe skillet and add butter. Add egg and cook as desired, readding medium-low heat as necessary. 
4. Top with cheese and cook until melted, about 1-2 minutes.
5. Place egg on top of sausage. Add desired condiments such as ketchup, add top muffin and press down, and serve.
